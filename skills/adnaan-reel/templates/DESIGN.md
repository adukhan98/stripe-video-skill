## Motion

{{MOTION}}

Defaults when the brand is silent (full rules: the skill's `references/animation-principles.md`):
- GSAP, one paused timeline, seek-safe and deterministic (no unseeded randomness).
- Strength **subtle** unless the project's SHARED.md says playful: anticipation 2–3 frames,
  overshoot 2–6 %, settle 6–10 frames.
- Ease out for entrances (`power3.out`, `back.out(1.2–1.6)` for pops), `power2.inOut` for moves,
  `power2.in` exits. Linear only for continuous mechanical motion.
- Entrances 8–18 frames, exits 6–9 frames (faster than entrances). One new idea at a time; parts of a
  group overlap 2–4 frames. Long moves travel on arcs.
- The contact frame lands ON the spoken word: start anticipation + main action that many frames before
  the word's timestamp.
- Holds ≥ 1 s are alive: slow push-in 100→103 %, drift, a single pulse. Never frozen.
- Logos: uniform scale, opacity and position only. No squash, stretch or rotation.
