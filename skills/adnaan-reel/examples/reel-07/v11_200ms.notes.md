- **Overshoot + settle** — pill 114 % → back.out + squash; hand overshoots 10° and settles on 72°; stopwatch body 108 % → back.out; "200" peak 1.1; browser card settle.

- **Squash/stretch on shapes only** — readout pill (shape) squash 6 % on the slam; never on type.
- **One focal point** — beat A: readout/stopwatch then words; hero: the red pill + "200" (the speaker card leaves, the page stays still); beat B end: the strip + arrow; the stopwatch only boils. No word is dimmed.
