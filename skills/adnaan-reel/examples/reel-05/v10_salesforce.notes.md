## Principles checklist

- Contact frames on words: every word starts 0.13–0.2 s early (runtime); chip, card 0, copies, ×3 and pills are timed so contact hits "Salesforce", "FDE", "team"/"in", "hiring".
- Anticipation: hero word sinks 3 f before rising; enter() dips the team card; copies lift off the corner before travelling; header compaction is power3.inOut.
- Overshoot + settle: "tripled" peaks 112 %, the others 105–106 %; cards and pills back.out; copies rotate past and settle.
- Parts lag the whole: avatars 3 f behind the card, label after; accent tint 2 f per avatar; header word lags the chip 2 f; pills 3 f apart; the original dips as the copies leave.
- Long moves on arcs: copies travel on curves (x bulge plus y with a different ease); speaker and page exits are short moves. The stack glide is 190 px, straight down with power ease (under the 200 px arc threshold).
- Squash/stretch on shapes only: team cards (3 %), ×3 pill, notification pills. Never on type, the logo (uniform pop only) or the speaker card.
- One focal point: page and speaker recede for "tripled"; the stack recedes for "every single other company"; the top phrase recedes when the pills take focus.
- Secondary action, no dead hold ≥ 1 s: grid drift, camera push, page scroll, card floats (staggered periods), dotted march, video push, pulsing dots (check: no dead holds).
- One exaggerated hero beat: "tripled" (peak 1.12 + 1 % camera punch + 4 f shake). Nothing else gets a punch.
- Exits faster than entrances: all exits are 6–7 f against entrances of 12–16 f; all finish before the last frame.
- One light direction / contact shadows: every shadow is the runtime's soft down-and-right card/pill shadow; no hard outlines.
- Not a static slide: something lands or moves on every beat, and the end frame is still living.

## Deviations

- The speaker card is 600x470, taller than the 432 runtime default, so the whole head fits a ≥ 600 card. It recedes during "tripled" and leaves at 2.28 (the brief only places it in beat A). This keeps the lower zone filled until the team copies arrive.
- The header compacts to one row instead of leaving; "Salesforce … tripled their FDE team" reads as one line.
- "company" uses `data-pop`, not `data-accent`. The runtime's data-accent entrance tweens `blur(8px→0)` with back.out(2.2), which overshoots to a negative blur. That is invalid CSS, so the word stays blurred while it is still settling (seen in a snapshot at 5.3 s). This is worth fixing in paper.js; I did not touch the runtime.
- "×3" and "Hiring: FDE" are labels the brief asked for. "their" and "them." are omitted.
