## As built (final render)

**What it shows:** full-screen opaque Ocean-editorial scene (paper + 60px grid + vignette, identical to v01_hook). Chapter: coin 03
(220px, white neumorphic, flips on rotY to the ocean face with deep-teal "03") + "third one" Newsreader 96 + "Layers" Fraunces
italic ocean 240. It compacts on an arc into a header that stays for the whole scene (coin 104px + "Layers" 118px, top-left).
Then seven soft axonometric slabs (SVG, projected rounded slabs: yaw -22 deg, elevation 30 deg, 740x200x64 plane units, light from
upper-right, soft drop shadows, ground contact shadow; white card material, top slab 07 ocean) stack bottom-up. Each carries its
REAL Layers-framework label on its front face (Geist 38 in face space, about 33px on screen; number Geist 600 muted or deep teal,
name Geist 500 ink): 01 Observed behaviour, 02 The domain, 03 User needs, 04 Product & service strategy, 05 Conceptual model,
06 Interaction structure & flow, 07 Surface. Then the stack eases to 88% and the black agent puck (196px, robot face, deep-teal visor,
ocean eyes) lands on Surface. An ocean compass needle swings out and settles pointing up-right, and an ink dotted path (3px, "2 12",
round caps) draws up-right to an ocean end-dot. Hard cut out, with no exit.
